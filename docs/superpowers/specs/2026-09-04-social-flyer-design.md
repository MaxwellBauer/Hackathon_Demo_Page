# INFINITE Social Flyer Design

## Goal

Create a polished 16:9 promotional flyer for LinkedIn and X that matches the website and sends viewers directly to the hackathon application.

## Deliverables

- An editable 1600 × 900 SVG source.
- A 1600 × 900 PNG export ready for social posting.
- Short LinkedIn and X captions containing a clickable application URL.

Files will live in `v2/assets/social/`.

## Visual Direction

Use the website's established black-and-gold system: near-black background, warm gold accents, ivory body copy, subtle grain, fine-line technical framing, and the infinity mark as the focal graphic. The composition should feel premium, scientific, and energetic rather than crowded.

The layout uses an asymmetric two-column structure. The left two-thirds hold the event identity and essential information. The right third contains a high-contrast application panel with a large QR code. Generous margins protect content from social-platform cropping.

## Copy Hierarchy

1. Eyebrow: `MIT MEDIA LAB · FALL 2026`
2. Brand: `∞ INFINITE`
3. Title: `THE INTERNET OF AGENTS HACKATHON`
4. Description: `Build decentralized agent swarms that tackle meaningful scientific and engineering problems.`
5. Event details: `OCT 30 – NOV 1, 2026` and `MIT MEDIA LAB · CAMBRIDGE, MA`
6. Call to action: `APPLY NOW`
7. Visible URL: `infinite-hackathon.vercel.app/apply.html`

The QR code must encode `https://infinite-hackathon.vercel.app/apply.html` exactly and include sufficient clear space for reliable scanning.

## Accessibility and Platform Safety

- Maintain strong contrast between type and background.
- Keep essential copy within a 96 px safe margin.
- Avoid small body text; the minimum final-size text is 24 px.
- Do not place text over visually noisy elements.
- Verify the QR code from the exported PNG, not only from the SVG source.

## Verification

- Confirm both exports are exactly 1600 × 900.
- Confirm the SVG is valid and the PNG renders without missing assets.
- Decode the QR code from the final PNG and verify the exact application URL.
- Visually inspect hierarchy, clipping, contrast, and spacing.
