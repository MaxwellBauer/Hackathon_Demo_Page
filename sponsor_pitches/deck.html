01 · Why now

The next leap in scientific AI is coordination.

Today’s most capable agents still operate inside isolated stacks. Science advances differently: specialized people, tools, datasets, simulations, and laboratories contribute distinct capabilities to a shared result.

Today

What teams build and validate

01

Problem

Choose a difficult, open-ended, consequential scientific or technical challenge.

02

System

Build a functioning decentralized agentic system—not only a concept or architecture.

03

Result

Demonstrate what the system actually accomplishes.

04

Validation

Support the result with evidence, benchmarks, comparison, or real-world validation.
